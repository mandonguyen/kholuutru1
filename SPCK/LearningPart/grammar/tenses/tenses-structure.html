Welcome to the present simple practice


Present Simple is usually use for permanent truths, regular habits, and fixed schedules


For examples:


  • Permanent truths: Water boils at 100 degree Celsius.
  • Regular habits: I walk to work every morning.
  • Fixed Schedules: The train leaves at 7:32 PM.


Stucture:


  • (+): S + V(s/es) + ...
  • (-): S + don't/doesn't + V + ...
  • (?): Do/Does + S + V + ...

  • Remember: the verb is infinitive form :D



For the structure:

  • "(+)" means affirmative form
  • "(-) means negative form"
  • "(?)" means question form

  • add e/es after the verb if the subject is he/she/is

And make sure you do the practice without helps outside!
